# Global Gaming Market Intelligence 🎮

**Video Game Sales Analytics | Power BI | DAX | Power Query**  
**Author / Portfolio Brand:** GCS Analytics

## Project Overview

Global Gaming Market Intelligence is a business intelligence project exploring video game sales across platforms, genres, regions, and release years. The project uses Power BI to present sales metrics and trends in an interactive dashboard.

## Business Objectives

- Compare total sales across gaming platforms and consoles.
- Understand regional sales distribution across North America, Europe/Africa (PAL), Japan, and other markets.
- Explore sales patterns by release year.
- Compare genre performance and identify areas for further investigation.
- Present findings in a clear, portfolio-ready BI report.

## Dataset

The report references the VGChartz video game sales dataset, with release years represented from 1977 to 2020. Metrics and findings in the report are based on the supplied dataset and existing dashboard/report. Final years may contain incomplete records, so recent-year declines should be interpreted cautiously.

## Tools and Skills

- **Power BI Desktop** — interactive report and data visualization
- **Power Query** — data preparation and transformation
- **DAX** — measures and KPI calculations
- **Analytics** — platform, genre, regional, and time-trend analysis

## Key Findings in the Current Report

- Reported global sales total approximately **$6.61B**.
- The **PS2** is ranked as the top console by reported sales.
- **North America** contributes approximately **50.68%** of reported global sales.
- The yearly sales trend peaks around **2008** in the dataset.
- Sports, Action, and Shooter are highlighted as leading genres in the report.

These are findings reported by the current project files; validate the underlying data model and measures before using the numbers in business decisions.
